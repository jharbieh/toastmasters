Opening

A New Toastmasters Year

Fellow Toastmasters, leaders, and club members.

Welcome to FY27. In front of us is a new Toastmasters year. A new beginning. A bright sunny day.

What will you do this year? What will you become? Do you have a goal in mind?

Intention

Remember Your Reason

You are spending your time here for a reason and I want you to remember it.

I want you to think about it each and every week because I want you to succeed and grow and become the best that you can be.

Inspiration

The Six Strings

I recently was asked to listen to Eric Church's commencement speech at UNC Chapel Hill.

He structured his speech around the six strings of a guitar.

  • Low E: Faith
  • A: Family
  • D: Love and partnership
  • G: Ambition and resilience
  • B: Community
  • High E: Individuality

Reflection

Retune Your Instrument

He reminded the graduates to always retune their strings and make sure that their instrument is in great shape.

He encouraged them to be original and true to themselves.

What does this mean for us as Toastmasters and members of this club, as individuals? What does this mean for you?

Action

How We Achieve Success

  1. Having successful meetings by participating and preparing for them
  2. Being intentional by specifically choosing to focus on one goal at a time each time
  3. Mentoring and coaching each other by asking if anyone needs help or calling someone

Action

Keep Going

  1. Supporting our club officers by doing our part
  2. Not being afraid to make mistakes
  3. Being free and feeling safe

Closing

Take Ownership of Your Destiny

Fellow Toastmasters, I want you to be successful.

I want you to grow and become the best version that you can be. I want you to shine this year.

I want you to take ownership of your destiny. I want you to be autonomous.
