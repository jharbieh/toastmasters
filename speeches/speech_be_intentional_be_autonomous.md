## The Six Strings

I recently was asked to listen to Eric Church's commencement speech at UNC Chapel Hill. Eric Church is a recording artist. He structured his speech around the six strings of a guitar.

- The Low E-string is about Faith
- The A string is about Family
- The D string is for love and partnership
- The G string is about ambition and resilience
- The B string is Community
- And finally, the high E string is individuality

He reminded the graduates to always retune their strings and make sure that their instrument is in great shape. He encouraged them to be original and true to themselves.

## What This Means for Us

What does this mean for us as Toastmasters and members of this club, as individuals? What does this mean for you?

In my opinion, for us, we can achieve success by having the following elements:

1. Having successful meetings by participating and preparing for them
2. Being intentional by specifically choosing to focus on one goal at a time each time
3. Mentoring and coaching each other by asking if anyone needs help or calling someone
4. Supporting our club officers by doing our part
5. Not being afraid to make mistakes
6. Being free and feeling safe
